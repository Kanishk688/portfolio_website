Margery Williams' nursery story in one volume — a toy rabbit, a boy, and the quiet turn from plaything to real.

**One volume, 20 pages**, all of them here — click any page to
read it at size. I also produced the **ElevenLabs voiceover** for this title;
that audio is not published here yet.

## What was hard about this one

The rabbit is a toy for most of the book and alive at the end, so the same character has to read as stitched fabric and then as living fur without becoming a different rabbit. The warm autumn key runs through the whole volume and had to hold from the nursery to the garden.

## Why the year matters

At the time, no mainstream image model could take a character and reliably
produce that same character in a new pose. There was no character reference,
no style lock, no consistent-character mode, and generated text was not clean
enough to print — which is why every speech bubble here is typeset over the
art rather than generated in it.

Today much of that is a solved problem. That is exactly why the work is dated:
the interesting part is not that the pages are consistent, it is what
consistency cost in 2025 — and that the pipeline built a year later for the
[Grade-5 science book](/work/jaipuria-science-grade5/) can now measure that
cost instead of quietly absorbing it.
