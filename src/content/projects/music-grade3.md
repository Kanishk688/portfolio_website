Every chapter of the Grade-3 music reader, made in October 2026: real-looking
Indian children cut out on a white page, Kiki the koel as the guide, and music
ideas such as notes, beats and melody shapes built as paper-craft objects.

## What was hard about this one

The book is printed as white pages with spot illustrations, so nothing could
lean on a painted background. Every image had to read on its own as a clean
cut-out, and the paper-craft notes and staircases had to look like something a
child could actually make in class.
