Five children's titles adapted as full colour comics — four Western classics
and one life of Mahavir — running to **ten volumes** in total. Twenty sample
pages are shown here; the complete books are linked above.

Each title needed a cast that stayed recognisable from cover to last page,
panel layouts that carried the story without the original prose, and a colour
key that shifted with the mood — the Mermaid's sunset against the sea witch's
cold cave, the Velveteen Rabbit's warm autumn against the nursery.

I also produced the **voiceovers for these titles in ElevenLabs**. Those audio
files are not published here yet.

## Why this looks easier than it was

This work is from 2025, and that matters more in generative media than in any
other kind of portfolio.

At the time, no mainstream image model could take a character and reliably
produce that same character in a new pose. There was no character reference,
no style lock, no consistent-character mode. A page like the Dodo's caucus
race, or the birth ceremony in the Mahavir book, meant generating many
candidates and rejecting most of them until the faces matched what had already
been printed on earlier pages.

The speech bubbles are typeset, not generated, for the same reason: models of
that period could not put clean, correct, legible text inside an image at print
quality.

Today a good chunk of this is a solved problem — the tools now hold a
character for you. That is the point of dating the work. **The interesting part
is not that the pages are consistent; it is what consistency cost in 2025, and
that the pipeline built for the [Grade-5 science book](/work/jaipuria-science-grade5/)
a year later can now measure exactly that cost instead of absorbing it.**
