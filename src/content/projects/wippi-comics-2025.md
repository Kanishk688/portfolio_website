Five children's titles adapted as full colour comics — four Western classics
and one life of Mahavir — across **ten volumes and 192 pages**. Every page
is here; click any of them to read at size.

Each title needed a cast that stayed recognisable from cover to last page,
panel layouts that carried the story without the original prose, and a colour
key that moved with the mood — the Mermaid's sunset against the sea witch's
cold cave, the Velveteen Rabbit's warm autumn against the nursery.

I also produced the **voiceovers for these titles in ElevenLabs**. Those audio
files are not published here yet.

## Why this looks easier than it was

This work is from 2025, and in generative media the year is part of the spec.

At the time, no mainstream image model could take a character and reliably
produce that same character in a new pose. There was no character reference,
no style lock, no consistent-character mode. A crowd page — the Dodo's caucus
race, the birth ceremony in the Mahavir book — meant generating many
candidates and rejecting most of them until the faces matched what had already
been printed on earlier pages.

The speech bubbles are typeset, not generated, for the same reason: models of
that period could not put clean, correct, legible text inside an image at
print quality.

Today much of this is a solved problem — the tools hold a character for you.
That is exactly why the work is dated. **The interesting part is not that the
pages are consistent; it is what consistency cost in 2025, and that the
pipeline built a year later for the
[Grade-5 science book](/work/jaipuria-science-grade5/) can now measure that
cost instead of quietly absorbing it.**
