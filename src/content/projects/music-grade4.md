Every chapter of the Grade-4 music reader, made in November 2026. Kiki the koel
carries over from the [Grade-3 book](/work/music-grade3/) so the two years feel
like one series, joined here by Mimi the monkey and Golu the elephant.

## What was hard about this one

Music is invisible, so every idea had to become an object a child can point at:
a melody as a pop-up garden, a scale as a staircase of paper blocks, a taal as a
wheel of eight bols. None of those graphics are reused from Grade 3; each was
designed fresh so the older book doesn't look like a reprint.
